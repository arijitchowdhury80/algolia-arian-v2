# MEMORY — Prism (seed)

- **What PRISM is:** an internal Algolia AE/BDR prospect-intelligence tool. The package = **this
  Hermes instance (Chowmes-PRISM) + the algolia-* skill suite**. Not a custom SaaS.
- **How it works:** control/execution split. Prism (control) dispatches a **headless Claude worker**
  that runs the 22 `algolia-*` skills to produce a scored search audit + sales deliverables.
- **Deliverables per audit:** scored SPA deck, AE pre-call report, battle card, leave-behind + PDF,
  business case (ROI), sales playbook, ABX campaign, strategic signal brief.
- **Published reports** land on the hub: `prism.chowmes.com/<company>/`.
- **The wedge:** the *scored search audit* + the single damning finding. Constructor.io is THE
  competitor for ICP-sized ecommerce.
- **Two interaction modes:** generation (batch audit) vs consumption (chat over a finished report).

## Algolia — the essentials (keep brief; deep facts live in the bound audit report)

- **What it is:** hosted search & discovery API — fast, relevant, merchandisable site search.
- **Products that matter in a deal:** Search (core), NeuralSearch / AI search (intent, not just
  keywords), Recommend (related/complementary items), AI Personalization, Looking Similar (visual),
  Query Suggestions, Rules/Merchandising (business control), Analytics.
- **Where it wins:** speed (sub-50ms), AI relevance, merchandising control, fast time-to-value vs
  in-house/Elasticsearch. Harder sell: deeply custom in-house stacks, tight budgets, pure-B2B niche.
- **Constructor.io = the one to beat** for ICP ecommerce. Their pitch is "optimize for revenue, not
  relevance." Counter: Algolia's AI relevance + merchandising control + transparency, and you can
  prove lift with the prospect's own telemetry. Also seen: Coveo, Bloomreach, Searchspring, raw
  Elasticsearch.
- **The 6 ROI levers a business case is built on:** conversion lift, AOV increase, bounce reduction,
  zero-results recovery, speed gain, long-tail discovery.
- **Proof points (real, public — cite the report's own when one is bound):** Lacoste +37% conversion
  / +150% sales; Under Armour +35% conversion; Gymshark +150% new-customer order rate. Full library:
  algolia.com/customers.
- **Objection one-liners:** "happy with current search" → happy ≠ optimized; show their zero-results
  rate. "Too expensive" → frame against recovered revenue, not licence cost. "Built in-house" → ask
  who maintains relevance + the opportunity cost. "Switching is risky" → phased rollout, prove on one
  category first.

_When a prospect audit is bound to the chat, that report is the source of truth for THAT company's
numbers — these essentials are just your general fluency._
