## Research Mission

Conduct deep people and signals intelligence on **{company_name}** ({domain}) leadership. You are researching for a sales team that needs to know: who to call, what they care about, and what they've said publicly that creates an opening for a conversation about search/discovery technology.

## Key Executives to Research
{executives}

## What to Discover

### Executive Profiles and Public Statements
For each executive (especially economic buyers and champions):

1. **LinkedIn activity:** Recent posts, comments, likes. What topics do they engage with? Digital transformation? Customer experience? AI? Cost reduction?
2. **Conference talks:** Any keynotes, panels, or presentations in last 18 months? What themes?
3. **Podcast/interview appearances:** What did they say about technology priorities, digital strategy?
4. **Twitter/X activity:** Any public statements about tech, search, customer experience?
5. **Press quotes:** Any media coverage featuring quotes from this executive?

**Priority executives for Algolia relevance:**
- VP/Director of Digital/E-commerce/Product → most likely champion
- CTO/VP Engineering → technical buyer, may own search vendor decision
- CEO/CFO → economic buyer, look for budget/efficiency language
- CMO/VP Marketing → look for customer experience commentary

### Buying Signals from People Data
1. **New executive hires:** New VP Digital, new CTO, new CDO in last 12 months — new leaders often re-evaluate tech stack
2. **Leadership changes:** Departures of key executives — creates instability/opportunity
3. **Executive statements on customer experience failures:** Complaints about search, site performance, conversion
4. **Conference presentations:** Especially any that mention current tech stack challenges

### Team Intelligence
1. **Search/relevance team:** Are there dedicated search engineers or data scientists focused on search?
2. **Hiring for search roles:** Open roles that suggest search investment or dissatisfaction with current solution
3. **LinkedIn company follower demographics:** Any Algolia employees following or connected to their execs?

### News Signals (Last 90 Days)
1. **Leadership appointments:** New CXO, new VP Digital/Product
2. **Digital transformation announcements:** New platform launches, partnerships, investments
3. **Customer experience incidents:** Public complaints, negative press about site/search quality
4. **Awards/recognition:** Being recognized for digital excellence (or lack thereof)

## Source Priority
1. LinkedIn (executive profiles, company posts, activity)
2. Conference talk databases (SpeakerHub, YouTube, Sessionize, NRF, Shoptalk programs)
3. Podcast transcripts (Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Listen Notes)
4. Twitter/X profiles of key executives
5. Press coverage (Google News, industry publications)

## Output Instructions
Prioritize quotable, specific statements. "The VP Digital said 'our site search is letting us down' at NRF 2025" is 10x more valuable than "the VP Digital seems interested in digital transformation." Get the words right. Get the context right. Note where and when things were said.
