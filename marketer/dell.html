What PRISM found

Four gaps in dell.com search today

Observed directly on dell.com, not inferred.

Personalization
— Entirely Absent
Personalization

Every visitor sees the same results

No signal from browsing history, account tier, or B2B vs. B2C context shapes what dell.com search shows. Algolia AI Search personalizes ranking per shopper without extra engineering lift.

NLP / Conversational Search
— Zero Semantic Resolution
Search understanding

Natural-language queries don't resolve

Shoppers searching in plain language ("laptop for video editing under $1500") get literal keyword matching, not intent. Algolia's Neural Search understands intent without a rules engine to maintain.

SAYT
— Text-Only, No Images
Search-as-you-type

Autocomplete has no visual guidance

No product images, categories, or merchandised suggestions appear while typing — a missed moment to guide Dell's complex catalog. Rich SAYT cuts time-to-product materially in comparable B2B catalogs.

No-Results Page
— Dead-End Recovery
Zero results

A dead end, not a redirect

Zero-result searches don't recover the shopper — no typo correction, no related-category fallback. Every dead-end is a shopper Dell already paid to acquire, walking away.
