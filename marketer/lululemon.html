What PRISM found

Gaps in lululemon athletica search today

Observed directly, not inferred.

semantic_nlp_search
semantic_nlp_search

Semantic / NLP FAIL: keyword `Ntt` engine cannot parse conversational intent

Typing a natural-language, price-scoped query ('yoga pants under 100') returned a completely empty 'Top Suggestions' overlay: the heading rendered with zero results beneath it. The keyword `Ntt` engine tokenizes words and cannot read 'under 100' as a price constraint or 'for hot yoga' as an activity/fabric signal, so intent queries fall through to nothing. Note the contrast with plain keyword terms, which do autocomplete richly (see F2/F4). Algolia NeuralSearch understands activity, price and feature intent natively — combining vector search with structured attribute filtering in a single query, no manual facet-setting required. This is the strongest wedge given the new CAITO's AI mandate.

query_suggestions_empty_state
query_suggestions_empty_state

Query suggestions are a static curated list, not demand-ranked or personalized

lululemon does populate the empty-state overlay: a 'Trending Searches' list renders (Belt Bag, Define Jacket, Align, Swiftly, Align Leggings) and returning guests also see a 'Recent Searches' block (screenshots 15 and 02). The gap is that this is a fixed, hand-curated list, not popularity ranked from real search demand, and it does not personalize to the individual shopper beyond simple recent history. So the highest-intent surface works, but it cannot flex to real-time demand, season or the specific guest. Algolia Query Suggestions ranks the overlay from live search analytics and refreshes automatically, and Personalization tailors it per shopper: the fixed curated list becomes a live, demand-ranked merchandising surface.

content_commerce_ux
content_commerce_ux

No federated search: help, service and policy content siloed from search

Search returns products but no service, help or policy content. Typing 'hemming' surfaces hem-named PRODUCTS (Nulu Cinch-Hem Pant, LuluLinen Adjustable-Hem Skirt, Cotton Jersey Bubble-Hem Tee, Notched-Hem Crop) plus a 'Curved Hem' suggestion, but never lululemon's free-hemming SERVICE or any policy/help page. That content lives in a separate Contentful layer (`cdn-contentful.lululemon.com`, content_type=cdp) and is not indexed into search, so the brand's signature service is invisible on the search surface. Algolia Federated Search unifies products, content, collections and stores in a single overlay, indexing the Contentful content lululemon already produces (including lululemon Studio content) alongside the apparel catalog.

intent_detection
intent_detection

Franchise terms autocomplete richly but do not route to curated, merchandised views

Brand and franchise terms do autocomplete richly: typing 'align' returns four Align product cards plus scoped query suggestions (Align Leggings, Align Bra, Align 25, Align Dress, Align 6 Shorts, Camo Align Leggings). The gap is downstream, not in autocomplete: those terms resolve to a generic keyword results page with no curated collection routing or merchandised landing, so a signature franchise gets matched, not merchandised. There is no intent categorization that sends 'run' to a curated Running view. Algolia Rules + query categorization route franchise, brand and activity terms to curated, filtered collection views: merchandised, not just matched.

search_intelligence
search_intelligence

Behavioral signal not fed into ranking: no popularity-ranked results or social proof

lululemon captures rich first-party behavior (Quantum Metric confirmed on site) and does surface recent searches and a trending list in the empty state (screenshot 02). What is missing is that this demand signal does not visibly rank what shoppers see: no bestseller or most-searched badges, no popularity-ranked result ordering, and no social-proof cues at the point of discovery. The signal is collected but not fed back into ranking. Algolia Analytics + insights-driven ranking turn behavioral data into popularity-ranked suggestions, trending terms and bestseller signals, closing the loop between the data lululemon already collects and what shoppers see.

typo_tolerance
typo_tolerance

Typo tolerance works: misspellings return correct results, but no explicit 'Did you mean' messaging

The correctly-spelled query autocompletes normally (screenshot 03 shows a populated 'leggings' SAYT), but no 'Did you mean…' correction UX was observed, and the misspelled variants ('lululemn leggings', 'align pnats') could not be captured on the results surface under the WAF block. Typo tolerance is therefore unconfirmed and flagged as a high-value gap for clean-IP re-test. Algolia typo tolerance is built in and requires zero configuration — misspellings resolve to the intended product automatically, with configurable 'Did you mean' prompts.

dynamic_facets_personalization
dynamic_facets_personalization

Static faceting & no visible personalization on a first-party-rich DTC model

Autocomplete surfaces recent searches, so there is some lightweight personalization at the SAYT layer, but facet adaptivity by category could not be enumerated under the WAF block and no 'Recommended for you' / personalized re-ranking of results was observed. lululemon's DTC first-party data + Quantum Metric behavioral capture are strong personalization inputs that the results surface does not appear to leverage. Flagged for clean-IP re-test. Algolia Dynamic Faceting adapts filters to category context, and Algolia Personalization re-ranks results using the first-party behavioral signal lululemon already captures.

recommendations_merchandising
recommendations_merchandising

Recommendations & merchandising rules siloed from search

PDP FBT/related carousels and search banners/curation could not be confirmed under the WAF block. Merchandising banners are handled by Contentful (`cdp` entries) separate from the `Ntt` search engine — indicating rules and content are not unified with search. Flagged for clean-IP re-test. Algolia Recommend (FBT / related / trending) plus the Rules engine unify recommendations and merchandising banners inside search — one ranking, one set of rules across search and browse.
